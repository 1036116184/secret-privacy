秘密 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 17 日

「秘密」是一款完全在用户设备本地运行的个人隐私数据管理工具，由周德全（Zhou Dequan）独立开发。核心原则：你的数据只保存在你的设备上，开发者无法访问，也不收集任何数据。

1. 我们不收集任何信息

本 App 没有账号系统，不需要注册；不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱或身份证信息；不包含广告、统计、埋点或任何第三方追踪 SDK；不会将你的任何内容上传到服务器；不读取通讯录、位置、通话记录、短信或相册中的其他内容。

2. 数据存储

你创建的文字、密码资料、分类以及导入的加密文件，全部仅存储在你的设备本地（App 沙盒目录内）。数据使用 AES-256-GCM 加密，密钥由你的密码通过 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256（120,000 次迭代）派生。开发者不持有你的密码，也无法解密你的数据；忘记密码将无法恢复。

3. 设备权限

  • Face ID / Touch ID（可选）：仅在你主动开启时用于本地解锁，生物识别信息由 iOS 处理，本 App 不读取、不存储。
  • 照片与文件（按需）：仅在你主动导入文件时调用系统选择器，选中的文件在本地加密保存。

4. App 内购买

高级版（com.mimi.secret.pro）通过 Apple App Store 内购完成，支付信息由 Apple 处理，开发者无法获取。详见 Apple 隐私政策。

5. 数据共享与第三方

我们不出售、不出租、不共享你的任何数据。本 App 未集成任何第三方广告、统计、社交或数据分析 SDK。

6. 数据保留与删除

数据保留时间由你决定。你可以在 App 内删除单条内容、清空某个界面，或通过「设置 → 通用 → 清空软件所有数据」删除全部数据。卸载 App 也会删除沙盒内全部数据。

7. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

8. 政策更新

政策变更时我们会更新本页面的「最后更新」日期，重大变更会在 App 内或本页面提示。

Secret · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Secret is a personal privacy data management tool that runs entirely on your device, developed by Zhou Dequan. Core principle: your data stays on your device; the Developer cannot access it and collects nothing.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. There is no account system or registration; no names, phone numbers, emails or IDs are collected; no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs; no content is uploaded to any server; contacts, location, call logs, messages and other photos are never accessed.

2. Data Storage

All notes, credentials, categories and imported encrypted files are stored only locally in the App sandbox. Data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM; keys are derived from your password via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (120,000 iterations). The Developer does not hold your password and cannot decrypt your data. Forgotten passwords cannot be recovered.

3. Device Permissions

  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional): used only for local unlock when you enable it. Biometric data is handled by iOS and never read or stored by the App.
  • Photos and Files (on demand): the system picker is invoked only when you import files; selected files are encrypted locally.

4. In-App Purchases

Pro (com.mimi.secret.pro) is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment information is processed by Apple and is not accessible to the Developer. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent or share your data. The App integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, social or data-processing SDKs.

6. Retention and Deletion

You decide how long data is kept. You can delete items or spaces in the App, or erase all data via Settings → General → Erase All Data. Uninstalling the App removes all sandbox data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Changes

We will update the "Last updated" date on this page when the policy changes; material changes will be highlighted in the App or on this page.
